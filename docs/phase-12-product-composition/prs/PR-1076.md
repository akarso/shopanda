# PR-1076 — Digital-Goods VAT/Tax Category

Status: planned

## Summary

- EU digital goods get the correct VAT treatment (reverse charge / OSS) rather than the physical-goods default.
- Extends the existing EU compliance module rather than adding a parallel tax path.

## Why

Digital goods have materially different VAT rules in the EU than physical goods (place-of-supply is the customer's location, OSS/MOSS reporting) — treating a `downloadable` product's tax the same as a `simple` physical product's would be a compliance bug, not just a modeling gap. This is the part of Track E most likely to need real tax-domain review, not just engineering review.

## Scope

- Consumes PR-1050's `RequiresPhysicalShipping = false` derivation to decide which tax category applies — `virtual` and `downloadable` both qualify, though confirm during implementation whether `virtual` (e.g. a service, not necessarily "digital goods" in the EU VAT sense) needs to be distinguished from `downloadable` specifically for tax purposes, since not every non-shipped product is EU-VAT "digital goods."
- Extend the existing EU compliance module (`docs/phase-5-maturity/specs/COMPLIANCE_EU.md`'s Omnibus/OSS groundwork) with the digital-goods category and place-of-supply rule, rather than a new tax subsystem.
- This PR needs explicit tax-domain sign-off before implementation starts — flag this in the actual PR when it's picked up, don't treat the roadmap description as sufficient specification for something with real compliance consequences.

## Out Of Scope

- Non-EU digital tax rules (US sales tax nexus for digital goods, etc.) — scoped to EU only, matching this codebase's existing compliance module's scope.

## Follow-up

- None further within Track E; this is a compliance-critical PR that should get its own careful review pass regardless of the rest of the track's velocity.
