# PR-1050 — Type-Driven Shipping/Tax Requirements

Status: planned

## Summary

- `virtual` and `downloadable` products never require a shipping method.
- Derive `RequiresPhysicalShipping bool` from `Type`, wired into the existing shipping-rate calculation.

## Why

Today every product implicitly requires shipping — there's no way to sell a virtual card or a digital download without checkout asking for/calculating a shipping method that doesn't apply. This is groundwork Track E's digital-goods VAT handling (PR-1076) also consumes; no VAT logic lives in this PR, just the shipping-requirement derivation.

## Scope

- `internal/domain/catalog` or `internal/domain/shipping` (wherever the derivation logically lives — decide during implementation, likely a method on `Product` or a small helper keyed off `Type`): `RequiresPhysicalShipping() bool` — `false` for `virtual`/`downloadable`, `true` otherwise (including `bundle`/`grouped`/`configurable`, since those compose from potentially-physical children — no rollup logic here, just the simple type-level default).
- Wire into checkout's shipping-rate step: a cart containing only non-physical-shipping items skips the shipping-method requirement entirely; a mixed cart still requires it (shipping applies to the physical items).
- Shipping cost calculation itself is unaffected — this PR only changes whether a shipping method is *required*, not how it's priced.

## Out Of Scope

- Bundle/configurable stock or shipping rollup from child products — Track C.
- Digital-goods VAT category — PR-1076, which consumes this PR's derivation.

## Follow-up

- PR-1076 (Track E) builds the VAT-category decision on top of `RequiresPhysicalShipping`.
