# PR-1063 — Stock Rollup Per Type

Status: planned

## Summary

- Bundle: `fixed` stock (own inventory row) or `dynamic` stock (minimum available quantity across required components) — merchant-configurable per bundle, mirroring PR-1062's pricing-mode split.
- Grouped: no parent stock; availability is per-member only.

## Why

Without an explicit rollup rule, "is this bundle in stock" has no defined answer — a bundle could show as purchasable while a required component is actually out of stock, or a merchant with true fixed inventory (a pre-assembled physical kit) would be forced into a rollup calculation that doesn't match reality.

## Scope

- `fixed` mode: bundle has its own stock quantity, tracked exactly like a `simple` product's stock — component stock is irrelevant to the bundle's own availability (matches a merchant who physically pre-assembles kits).
- `dynamic` mode: bundle's available quantity = `min(available_qty(component) / quantity_required(component))` across all `required = true` components; optional components don't gate availability.
- Confirm interaction with reservation handling (`internal/domain/inventory`) — a dynamic bundle's "reservation" needs to reserve the underlying components' stock, not a separate bundle-level stock row, or the rollup calculation and the reservation system will disagree with each other under concurrent checkout load.

## Out Of Scope

- Order-line explosion (does a bundle sale decrement the bundle's own stock or its components' stock in the order/fulfillment record) — PR-1064.

## Follow-up

- PR-1064 — order line explosion, which is where stock *decrement* (not just availability check) needs to be consistent with this PR's chosen rollup mode.
