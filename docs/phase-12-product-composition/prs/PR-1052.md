# PR-1052 — CSV Import/Export: Type Field

Status: planned

## Summary

- Extend the existing product import/export pipeline with the `type` column.

## Why

Every other product field has CSV import/export support (`internal/application/importer`, `internal/application/exporter`); `type` shouldn't be the exception, or bulk catalog operations regress the moment this field starts mattering.

## Scope

- `internal/application/importer/products.go` (or equivalent): parse/validate `type` column, same error-reporting shape as other field validation failures (row number, column, reason).
- `internal/application/exporter/products.go`: include `type` in the export.
- Unknown/invalid type value: rejected per-row with a clear message, matching existing importer behavior — not a whole-file failure.

## Out Of Scope

- Type-specific composition data (bundle components, linked eligibility, etc.) — those get their own import/export columns/files in their respective tracks (PR-1066, PR-1073, PR-1078), consolidated for consistency in PR-1081.

## Follow-up

- PR-1081 (Track F) is the final consistency pass across every field this phase adds.
